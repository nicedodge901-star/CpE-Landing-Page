About Computer Engineering

Computer Engineering joins electronics and computing: designing the hardware computers run on, the software that powers it, and the networks that connect them. That range opens many paths. Graduates become software engineers, cybersecurity specialists who protect systems and data, IT professionals who run infrastructure and support, or educators and researchers who train the next generation of engineers. Whichever you choose, the foundation is the same: a problem-solving mindset and the ability to build working systems from end to end.
